## Retention: GOOD vs MANIPULATIVE
GOOD: open promise due Fri; couch ends Mon; “who told landlord?” mystery; Musa remembers greeting; levy outcome visible; cousin room unlocks via kept word; callbacks (“Tobi housed you → asks ₦2k”). All derive from player history and change options.
MANIPULATIVE (banned): loot boxes, gambling odds, fake scarcity timers, streak-breaking threats, energy paywalls, spam notifications, pay-to-clear-standing. Detection rule: if retention requires payment or punishes absence (offline eviction), reject.
