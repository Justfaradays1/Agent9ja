# AGENT9JA_CORE_FUN_RED_TEAM.md
*Hostile review. No code. No UI. No expansion.*

## Verdict on Previous Slice
Slice is well-constructed and too perfect. Hidden cause + conflicting claims + verify + promise + callback reads as neon INVESTIGATE HERE. Tola scenario would demo well and die by Day 3. Core hypothesis “will players spend to verify?” is wrong question. Players will verify once. They won't pay to verify forever. Mystery is consumable. What repeats is **what you do after you know truth.**

## Strongest Argument Against Social Investigation
Investigation is a tutorial verb, not a loop. Papers Please works because inspect→decide repeats with new moral weights every 60 seconds. Our verify repeats with same shape: ask second source, catch lie, feel smart. By third agent player has seen pattern. No new tension. Luck vs cleverness collapses because Musa-truth-table makes answer deterministic. If Kunle-known-lying kills fun, we built mystery not strategy. Real repeatable fun in our material is not “who lies?” but **“now I know, who do I owe/use/expose/protect, and what does it cost me?”** Obligations, host clocks, levies, ajo, black tax repeat. Verification doesn't.

## Competing Core Loops (ranked)
1. **Manage obligations/leverage (strongest).** Do: owe Tobi Fri, owe Sunday dash, owe landlord respect. Decision: who to disappoint. Tension: every help creates claim. Surprise: call-ins. Replay: different debt graphs. Retention: promises due.
2. **Survive compound politics.** Do: pay/refuse levy, pick side in gossip, overhear. Tension: shared costs + reputation propagation. Replay: different standing. Retention: meeting rhythm.
3. **Ask for help / Find Help.** Do: choose who to ask, public vs private. Tension: signalling need creates debt. Surprise: who actually shows. Replay: network variance. Retention: favour half-lives.
4. **Resource sacrifice.** Do: ₦500 verify vs food vs dash vs save. Tension: opportunity cost with people attached. Replay: scarcity seeds. Retention: runway clock. Needs people to matter — alone it's arithmetic.
5. **Reputation.** Do: vouch/warn/refuse. Tension: spend rep. Surprise: tags follow. Replay: notorious vs trusted runs. Retention: doors open/close.
6. **Recognition/humour.** Do: collect wahala stories. Tension: none. Surprise: twist. Replay: low. Retention: memes fade. Fuel, not engine.
7. **Social investigation (current).** Do: compare claims. Tension: uncertainty. Surprise: lie revealed. Replay: weak — same trick. Retention: weakest after 3 solves. **Rank 7/7 as loop, rank 1/7 as hook.**

## Verification Red-Team
A (told contradiction → click Musa → answer) is admin. B (feel wrong → spend → new contradiction → decide trust) is game. Minimum for B: 2 claims, 2 sources with different incentives, 1 cost, 1 follow-on contradiction, no free oracle. Musa must be able to mislead while truthful (doesn't know tenant agreed to leave tomorrow). Verification fun = judging people under cost, not collecting facts. If answer always Musa, no game.

## Wahala Board Red-Team
A Full board: clarity high, immersion low, admin feel, cheap-phone heavy. B “Something no add up” card: suspicion-triggered, low load, high agency. C No UI, memory only: immersive, but loses track, frustrating on return. **REC: B for slice, kill A.** Board should appear only on felt contradiction, show 3 lines max + 1 suggested verify with cost. If players never open B, investigation isn't wanted — kill loop.

## Time System Red-Team
4 slots is convenient spreadsheet, not fun. A slots: clear tradeoffs, gamey, calendar feel. B continuous/deadlines: natural (“room gone by 4”, “Tobi needs answer tonight”), harder to balance. C no time, only commitments: simplest, social clocks drive. D relationship clocks: strongest for our theme. **REC: D + lightweight day phase (morning/night) for slice.** Cut 4-slot grid. Test: do players feel “can't do both” without counting slots?

## Money Red-Team
₦8k only matters if no dominant choice. Three non-obvious trilemmas:
- ₦500 verify uncertain room vs ₦500 food 2 days vs ₦500 dash Sunday for future tip. Optimal depends on who you believe, not math.
- ₦1.5k food contribution to keep couch vs ₦1.5k transport for 2 inspections. Depends on Tobi patience vs market odds.
- ₦1k levy (standing) vs save for deposit (runway). Depends on compound power vs landlord mood.
If one path always wins, money is flavour. Make costs social: every spend is also a signal to someone.

## Luck vs Cleverness
A must beat B reliably. Clever edge from: incentive literacy (agent margin rises with rent → hikes suspect), consistency (Kunle swaps guy on day), history (Sunday skimmed before), timing (Musa 6am vs 2pm differs), corroboration (2 independent sources), rep (Seyi burned before = careful). No dice-gated truth. Wrong guess must come from misjudging people, not RNG. Test: A win-rate ≥65% over 3 runs vs B ~35%, else no skill.

## NPC Truthfulness Model
“Musa never lies about occupancy” makes him database. Replace with: incentives + boundaries + bias + loyalty + fear + misunderstanding. Musa truthful about seen occupancy, ignorant of tomorrow's private agreement, loyal to caretaker for job, fears landlord. So “tenant never comot” true-now, misleading-next. Every oracle fallible outside boundary. Document per NPC: knows / doesn't know / wants / hides / fears / misreads. Deterministic lookup dead; judged reliability lives.

## Player Deception Test
A NPC-only lies: simpler, safe, enough for slice. B both lie: richer but moderation/complexity + fraud-tutorial risk. C misrepresent intentions only (can't fabricate facts): middle. **Keep A for slice.** Player lie (bank job → which branch?) is nice callback but not core. Cut player fabrication until social strategy proven. Lie about intent (promise you may break) already gives deception via promises.

## Memory vs Consequence
Without memory, 30 min still works as mystery — proves memory not hook. With memory, new verbs unlock: called-in favours, vouches priced by history, doors closed by scars, Book references. 5 memory-gated futures:
1 Kept Tobi Fri → vouches → +7 days.
2 Ghosted Musa greeting → refuses 6am confirm.
3 Paid Sunday dash → early tip next crisis.
4 Accused without proof → Adewale cools, paper requests blocked.
5 Helped Seyi levy → corroborates price vs agent later.
**Consequence is moat, memory is storage.** Memory without changing options is trivia. Ship only remembered things that change options, and show them.

## Compound Necessity Test
Replace compound with random city NPCs → game collapses to fetch quests. Compound indispensable for: repeated encounters, overhearing, shared costs, group chat public memory, rep propagation, recurring faces, infrastructure (light/water/gate). Minimum: 6 residents + 1 shared cost + 1 group chat + Book + overhear spot. One function if forced: **people you can't avoid tomorrow.** That's why promises matter.

## Control Version A — No Investigation
Ask → choose → manage rels → consequences, no lies/board/hidden cause. All prices true, agents honest but expensive/rude/slow. Fun remains: host clocks, levy splits, promise triage, favour routing. Likely **more robust than mystery version** for retention. If A ≈ B on fun, we over-engineered. HYP: A retains better past Day 3.

## Control Version B — No Housing
Compound obligations only: levies, water expiring, Tobi needs ₦2k, Seyi vs Sunday feud, ajo invite. Still Agent9ja? **Yes for 30 min, no for hook.** Housing gives legible countdown + visual move + universal dread. Without it, stakes diffuse. Conclusion: housing is best Act I trigger, not whole game. Keep as opener, prove compound politics carries Act II.

## Smallest True Gameplay Loop
**Hear conflict → judge who needs you → spend scarce favour/money → owe/be owed.**
4 verbs: **Ask / Promise / Vouch / Repay.** Verify folds into Ask (with cost). Decide folds into Promise/Vouch.

## Redesigned First Session (player-driven, not scripted)
Start: ₦8k, couch 3 nights (Tobi brother Tue), 14-day notice (cause hidden among 3 variants), 6 contacts, Day phase AM/PM only. You know budget + deadline + who claims what initially. Hidden: cause + which contact flaky (randomized). Pressures: if do nothing, couch ends + notice ticks + Friday food promise auto-created if you eat. Actions: ask anyone, verify any way (costs), work shift (misses visit), promise/vouch/refuse. World reacts via ledger + temps + Book. No forced Kunle path — player may never meet Kunle and still finish via Musa/Seyi/cousin. Authored conditions, emergent sequence.

## Procedural Variation Test
Randomize liar (Kunle/Sunday/Seyi-stressed), cause (son/agent skim/complaint/sale), who needs money (Tobi/Musa), which room real, warm/cold ties. Survives if systems (costly verify, favour ledger, rep gates, clocks) still produce dilemma + discovery + debt. If breaks, we have narrative not sim. Minimum for survival: incentive-tagged NPCs + 2 independent sources per key claim + favour costs + clocks + memory callbacks.

## Mystery vs Social Strategy
Mystery: who lies? One-time dopamine, dead on replay, dies when answer known. Strategy: now I know, expose/use/save/leverage whom at what relationship cost? Replays, retains, generates stories. **Heart must be social strategy.** Mystery is hook for 10 min. Design for known-truth play: expose Kunle (lose future leads, gain Seyi trust) vs use him (pay cut, keep pipeline) vs tell Sunday (war) vs bank leverage for Adewale extra days.

## What Must Be Built First
Suspicion card + 1 costly verify + promise/debt ledger with call-ins + 6 incentive-tagged NPCs + compound group + played-days clocks + share card. Nothing else.

## What Must NOT Be Built Yet
Board-full, slots grid, ajo/black-tax systems, romance, market, careers, city rings, plaza, ghosts, Daily, Gist, ads, real housing, AI chat, scores, streaks.

## Kill Criteria
- <40% verify voluntarily in pilot.
- Players can't name who lied + why without prompt.
- Clever play win-rate ≈ random.
- No unprompted screenshot/retell with because.
- Day-3 return intent <30%.
- Board open rate <25% or “spreadsheet” feedback.
- Known-answer replay rated boring.

## Final Recommendation
PIVOT
Social investigation as mystery-loop will demo clever and die by week two — one pattern, deterministic oracles, consumable surprise, spreadsheet temptation. Stronger loop already in our material is obligation/leverage strategy in a dense compound where known truth still forces hard social choices about who to disappoint, protect, or spend reputation on. Pivot slice to known-truth decisions + promise/debt/call-in memory, keep verification as single suspicion hook, cut board/slots/economy to minimum, and prove players manage people not puzzles before building any world.
