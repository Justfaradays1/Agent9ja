# AGENT9JA_CORE_FUN_VERTICAL_SLICE.md
*Lead design + skeptical CTO. Compress, don't expand. No code. No UI code.*

## 1. Executive Verdict
Agent9ja is fun **iff** the player feels clever for figuring out who to trust, not for finding a house. The fun is: **hear conflicting claims → suspect something → verify cheaply → decide under time/money pressure → be remembered for it.**

If the first 30 minutes don't produce “Wait, I need to figure out who's telling truth” + “They remember what I did,” more systems won't save it. Housing is the right Act I pressure because it forces you into people; it is not the game. The game underneath is **social investigation with memory.**

Blunt: current 30-system design is a good document, weak game. Cut to 1 compound, 6 NPCs, 1 deception chain, 1 promise, 1 debt, 1 verification, 1 consequence. Prove cleverness > luck.

## 2. What Agent9ja Actually Is
A 30-minute phone-first social-investigation sim: broke newcomer with 14 days to secure a bed, 6 people who know different things, ₦8,000 that can't cover everything, one lie that unravels, and a compound that remembers. Not a listings app. Not Sims. Not Lagos Life plaza.

## 3. Core Fun Hypothesis
**HYP:** Fun = suspicion → cheap verification → risky decision → visible memory.
Player does `Ask → Verify → Decide → Live consequence` every 3-5 min. Cleverness beats luck because tells are consistent and second sources exist. Test: do players voluntarily investigate without prompt and retell with “because”?

## 4. What We Are Testing
- Will players suspect without being told?
- Will they spend scarce time/money to verify?
- Do they care when NPC references past act?
- Do they feel clever not lucky?
- Do they want minute 31?

## 5. What We Are NOT Testing
City, plaza, multiplayer, ghosts, Daily Wahala, Gist, elections, businesses, romance, careers, inventory, progression trees, economy sim, ads, real housing, creator tools, AI free-text.

## 6. First 30 Minutes — Detailed Walkthrough
Setup: You are Tola, final-year student. Hostel closed. ₦8,000. Bag + phone. 14 days to find bed. Compound 12, Yaba-like. Contacts: Tobi (friend), Sunday (caretaker), Musa (gateman), Mr Adewale (landlord), Kunle (agent), Seyi (tenant).

### Beat 0 — Buzz (0:00-0:45)
- Wants: know why landlord summons.
- Info: voice note Sunday “Oga want see you, e no be small” + landlord “Come today.”
- Do: pick reply tone Polite / Straight / Beg.
- With: Sunday, Adewale (async).
- NPC wants: Adewale tests respect; Sunday wants relevance.
- Believes: routine warning.
- True: 14-day notice coming, cause hidden (agent pitched higher tenant).
- Choice: tone.
- Changes: tone tag; Polite opens negotiation later, Beg shows need, Straight earns wary respect.
- Remembers: Adewale notes first impression.
- Unlocks: meeting. Why care: threat + voice human.

### Beat 1 — Notice (2:00-6:00)
- Wants: keep roof.
- Info: “29 days? No — 14 days. My son is returning.” Group chat explodes: Seyi “Wahala dey.”
- Do: choose Life Card detail (already dealt): Final-year, ₦40k debt to school? No — keep simple: ₦8k, no job yet.
- NPC wants: Adewale wants room for son (claimed); Sunday wants cut.
- Believes: son story.
- True: partial — son real but not returning soon; Sunday invited richer tenant.
- Choice: ask Adewale “why me?” vs stay silent.
- Changes: asking unlocks cause-thread; silence keeps landlord warm but blind.
- Remembers: group saw your question.
- Unlocks: Find Help. Care: countdown + mystery seed.

### Beat 2 — OH DIFFERENT moment (6:00-12:00)
Kunle DMs uninvited: “I get one for you, ₦3k inspection, pay now or gone.” Tobi offers couch 3 nights free. Sunday hints “I fit help, for drink.”
- Wants: bed tonight + lead.
- Info: 3 conflicting paths, all cost different currency (money vs favour vs obligation).
- Do: choose one to pursue first (only time for one today).
- NPC wants: Kunle fee now; Tobi loyalty; Sunday dash.
- Believes: Kunle fastest.
- True: Kunle has no mandate; shows different house; “taken.”
- Choice: pay Kunle vs take couch vs tip Sunday N500.
- Changes: money -₦3k or promise to Tobi or Sunday tip → different info unlocked.
- Remembers: ledger entry + who you trusted first.
- Unlocks: verification need. Care: first real loss possible. **Signature: two people tell opposite things about same room.**

### Beat 3 — Verification (12:00-20:00)
You visit with Kunle's guy. House ≠ pics (“sample/potential”). Gateman Musa: “This room? Tenant never comot o.” Tenant Seyi: “We pay ₦450k, not ₦500k agent talk.”
- Wants: know true price/availability.
- Info: Wahala Board auto-opens ONLY on contradiction (not checklist): Agent ₦500k / Available now vs Musa Occupied vs Seyi ₦450k.
- Do: pick 1 verify: ask group (public, costs standing) / visit 6am (time slot) / request paper (needs landlord warmth) / send Tobi (owes favour).
- NPC wants: Musa wants respect + small dash; Seyi wants ally vs landlord.
- Believes: confused.
- True: room occupied 7 more days; Kunle chain 3 links; Sunday promised it to another.
- Choice: which verify.
- Changes: reveals chain length + true rent + Sunday role.
- Remembers: who you asked, who you bypassed (Kunle anger if bypassed).
- Unlocks: decision with leverage. Care: cleverness pays.

### Beat 4 — Promise/Debt (20:00-26:00)
Tobi: “My brother dey come Tuesday, you fit stay till Monday.” Needs food contribution ₦1,500. Work shift available same evening ₦4k but clashes with 6am visit.
- Wants: shelter + money.
- Info: can't do both shift + early visit (time slots).
- Do: promise Tobi pay Friday + chores vs take shift and risk couch.
- NPC wants: Tobi wants reliability; boss wants shift covered.
- Believes: can do both.
- True: energy soft cap — doing both = too tired to bargain tomorrow (-bargain option).
- Choice: promise vs shift.
- Changes: promise object (to Tobi, Fri, witnessed by group) OR +₦4k -shelter security.
- Remembers: Tobi ledger; boss notes reliability.
- Unlocks: landlord negotiation leverage if you kept promise (Tobi vouches). Care: sacrifice who?

### Beat 5 — Decision + Consequence (26:00-30:00)
Confront Adewale with what you learned (optional): “Sunday don show room to another?” If Polite history + evidence (paper or 2 sources), Adewale pauses eviction 7 extra days + warns Sunday. If no evidence + accusation, Adewale cools, Sunday blocks future tips.
- Wants: time.
- Info: what you verified.
- Do: negotiate / beg stay / accept couch and hunt cousin room.
- NPC wants: Adewale wants control + no shame; Sunday wants cut preserved.
- Believes: son story.
- True: son delay real; Sunday hustle real.
- Choice: leverage vs plead vs walk.
- Changes: +7 days OR +room lead OR +enemy.
- Remembers: Book entry “Tola questioned notice Day 1.”
- Unlocks: minute-31 thread: who told landlord what? + Friday promise due. Care: agency, not luck.

Core loop: **ask → verify → decide → live consequence.** Alternatives rejected: hustle-connect-negotiate-survive is too generic; lacks suspicion trigger.

## 8. NPC Cast (6, memorable for one thing)
1. **Mr Adewale (landlord):** goal keep control + son story; pressure family abroad; knows true rent/future; wants respect; lies about urgency, never about rules; remembers tone + if challenged with proof; attitude shifts with evidence + politeness; can grant +7 days or cool. Remembered as “typing… then 14 days.”
2. **Sunday (caretaker):** goal cut + relevance; pressure landlord + tenants; knows true state/mood/real rent/skim; wants dash/favours; lies about availability/cause, never about compound mechanics; remembers tips vs bypass; can open rooms or block. Remembered as voice note + “for drink.”
3. **Musa (gateman):** goal quiet shift + respect; knows who lives/visits/owner patterns; wants greeting/dash; never lies about occupancy, omits landlord gist; remembers greeting; can confirm occupancy (killer verify). Remembered as “tenant never comot o.”
4. **Tobi (friend):** goal loyalty test; pressure brother return; knows your history; wants promise kept + chores; lies never about shelter deadline; remembers promise Fri; can vouch or withdraw couch. Remembered as couch + Tuesday deadline.
5. **Kunle (agent chain):** goal fee now; pressure quota; knows listings/other agents; wants pay today; lies about mandate/availability, never about fee amount; remembers bypass; can ghost. Remembered as “house don go but I get another.”
6. **Seyi (tenant):** goal ally vs landlord; pressure rent; knows lived reality/true cost; wants help with levy; lies never about price paid; remembers if you quoted her; can corroborate. Remembered as ₦450k not ₦500k.

## 9. Information / Verification System
Board appears ONLY on contradiction, shows claim/source/stake/confidence. Verifies cost: group (standing), 6am (time), paper (warmth), send friend (favour), small test ₦500 (money). “Something no add up” flag, not spreadsheet. If board feels admin, cut to 3-line suspicion card — test open rate.

## 10. Deception Chain
NPC→player: Kunle available-now lie → Musa occupied truth → Seyi price truth → Sunday cause lie. Player→NPC: lie “I work bank” → Adewale “which branch?” → later shift boss mentions no-show → Adewale cools + tag “Talks big.” Consequence social, not fraud tutorial. One lie enough for slice.

## 11. Memory & Consequences (3 callbacks in slice)
1. Tone Polite → Adewale allows paper request; Beg → extra pity tip from Seyi but Adewale doubts pay ability.
2. Promise Tobi Fri → Day 3 Tobi asks early “abeg send half today?” + group notes; if kept, Tobi vouches to Adewale (+7 days); if broken, Frosty + Sunday refuses tip (“you sef dey owe”).
3. Bypass Kunle via Musa → Kunle ghosts + spreads “Tola no serious” (one tenant cold); but Musa warms and reveals 6am truth. Player thinks “they remember.”

## 12. Housing Problem (Act I, minimal)
14 days, couch 3 nights, cousin room needs deposit Mon, current room Shaky. States used: Shaky → Borrowed → Shared-lead. No apartment purchase. Tests dependence, not search skill.

## 13. Money Problem (minimal)
₦8,000 start. Costs: inspection 3k, dash 500, food 1.5k, transport 2×800, levy 1k, shift +4k opportunity. Can't cover all. Choice is sacrifice + who affected. No savings/invest/ajo in slice (ajo hint only).

## 14. Compound Gameplay (2 examples)
1. Overhear at tap: Musa + Seyi argue pump fuel ₦11k/mo vs Band-A ₦310 — reveals caretaker cut + gives verify lever (tell tenants? costs Sunday enmity).
2. Levy poll in group: gen ₦1k each. Pay → Harmony up + Sunday owes; refuse → standing down but save for inspection. Both remembered in Book.

## 15. Emotional Journey
Buzz/anxiety → confusion → suspicion → curiosity → agency (verify) → tension (promise) → consequence (relief or sting) → “what about Friday/cause?” No crisis pile; humour beats: sample house, typing…Ok, UP NEPA shout when light returns mid-visit.

## 16. Minute-31 Hook
Unresolved: Friday promise due + hidden cause teaser (“Mariam saw Sunday with stranger”) + couch ends Monday. Strongest: **“I need to know who told landlord that + I promised.”** Obligation + mystery > loot.

## 17. Day-3 Retention Hypothesis
Promise due, couch clock, 6am visit payoff, group reaction to choice, shift vs hunt tradeoff. Return for people + debt, not coins. Test Day-3 return after first crisis.

## 18. Low-Data / Cheap-Phone
Text-first, <2MB, link-play, no real-time, async turns, voice-note optional compressed, staged offline loss, played-days clock, no heavy anim, interrupt-proof (NEPA/data cut safe). Fun survives because decisions are async + memory persists.

## 23. Prototype Specification
1 player, 1 compound 6 NPCs, 14-day clock (30-min demo compresses to 3 days), ₦8k, 4 time slots/day, wallet send/lend/split, 2 jobs (shift/gig), group + DMs (menu choices + tone), Board suspicion card, ledger/promises, 15 storylets + 4 cause variants, 3 memory callbacks, share story card. End: housed-temp or +7 days + Friday debt + cause thread. Open thread = promise + mystery.

## 24. Biggest Risks
Board feels spreadsheet; verification costs annoying; NPCs feel bots; lie has no teeth; money trivial; housing solved too fast/slow; visual-novel feel (no agency); luck > clever; phone text wall; post-crisis void.

## 25. What Could Kill Agent9ja
Shallow social, stress without joy, unclear verb, luck economy, empty chat, fake counts exposed, stereotype humour, clone feel, Day-3 churn, ad damage. Single biggest unknown: **will players spend scarce slot to verify vs take first offer?** If no, no game.

## 26. Final Recommendation
Build WhatsApp roleplay 15-person 7-day first (you as landlord/caretaker/agent/gateman). Measure investigate rate, screenshots, return, “because” retells. If ≥60% investigate + ≥30% share unprompted + Day-7 return, build link-play slice above. Else kill investigation thesis and pivot to pure social survival (help/debt/compound) without deception.

**If I were responsible for Agent9ja tomorrow, I would build the 14-day quit-notice + conflicting claims + one cheap verify + promise ledger first, because cleverness about people is the only fun that survives cheap phones, 10 users, and no plaza.**
